Why a good towel matters

You use it every day. It should feel like it.

Few things you own touch your skin as often as a towel, and almost nobody thinks about it. That is a shame, because the difference between an ordinary towel and a truly good one is something you feel every single morning.

  1. 01

    It is the first thing your skin feels

    After a bath your skin is warm, damp and at its most sensitive. Long, smooth cotton fibres glide over it. Short, coarse ones catch and scratch, and you notice it even if you never put it into words.

  2. 02

    It should actually dry you

    Absorbency is the whole point. Dense loops of good cotton draw water away in one gentle pass, instead of pushing it around your skin while you rub harder.

  3. 03

    Softness that breathes

    A great towel feels soft without feeling heavy or clammy. Natural cotton lets air move through it, so it dries between uses and smells fresh rather than damp.

  4. 04

    Why cheap towels let you down

    Short fibres and thin construction shed lint, stiffen after a few washes and lose their loops. Many are coated with softeners that feel plush in the shop and wash out within weeks.

  5. 05

    Better cotton lasts longer

    Long-staple fibres spin into stronger, smoother yarn. The towel keeps its loft, its colour and its softness for years rather than months, so it is the better buy as well as the better feeling.

  6. 06

    A small luxury, every day

    You will reach for it hundreds of times a year. Very little else you own is used so often, or can change how a morning feels so easily.

The Aegean cotton story

It begins in the fields above the Aegean.

Between the mountains and the sea of western Turkey, long sunlit summers and fertile river valleys have grown cotton for centuries. The fibre that comes from them is long, fine and strong, and it is the reason a Turkish towel feels the way it does.

  • Softness

    Long fibres leave fewer loose ends in every thread, so the surface stays smooth. It feels silky against the skin from the very first use.

  • Long fibres

    Long-staple cotton spins into fine, even yarn that holds together. That means less lint, less pilling and loops that keep their shape.

  • Absorbency

    Cotton drinks water naturally. Woven into dense loops, it draws moisture from the skin quickly and holds it, so one towel is all you need.

  • Breathability

    Air moves freely through natural cotton. Your towel dries between uses and stays fresh, instead of hanging damp on the rail.

  • Durability

    Strong yarn and a tight weave stand up to hundreds of washes, keeping their softness and their colour year after year.

Woven in Denizli

A few hours inland from the Aegean coast, Denizli makes most of Turkey's towels and has woven cloth for generations. Ours are woven there by a family workshop, finished by hand and checked one by one before they leave.

We never rush the loom, never coat the cotton in softeners and never cut corners on the hem. What reaches you is simply very good cotton, woven well.

Most people never realise a towel can make this much difference. Until the first morning they use one.
