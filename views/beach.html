What is a peshtemal?

The original Turkish towel

For centuries the peshtemal was the cloth of the Turkish hammam: thin, flat woven cotton, finished with a knotted fringe. Today it goes from the bathroom to the beach, the pool and the boat deck, and it is the towel most Americans picture when they hear "Turkish towel".
