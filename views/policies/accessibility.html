We want everyone to be able to browse and buy from Pamuq, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. That includes text alternatives for images, visible keyboard focus, labelled form fields, sufficient color contrast, menus and dialogs that work from the keyboard, and respecting your device's reduced motion setting.

Known limitations

Checkout is hosted by Stripe, which maintains its own accessibility standards. Some product photographs are still being replaced, and their descriptions will be updated with them.
